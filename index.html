Quick guide

Start with your maximum loss

Enter the most you plan to lose in rupiah, including buy and sell fees. Tap a shortcut to use a saved amount.

Choose a mode

Single entry
Enter your entry and stop-loss prices. The calculator rounds down to whole lots so the planned loss stays within your limit. One lot is 100 shares.
DCA entries
Set two or three entry prices, assign capital percentages totaling 100%, and choose one shared stop. Shortcuts change with the number of entries.
Calculate SL
Enter your entry price and a fixed lot quantity. The note beside Position size shows the minimum exit price needed to stay within your loss limit, followed by its price-change percentage. The calculated exit rounds upward to whole rupiah. Verify your broker’s valid price increments.

Test which DCA orders fill

Check or uncheck 01 / 02 / 03, or use the matching results-panel checklist. Results include checked entries only. Each order keeps its original planned lots; unchecking an entry does not give its allocation to another order.

Read the small notes

Break-even beside entry price
The selling price needed to cover that entry’s buy and sell fees. In DCA, each entry has its own note; the result summary shows break-even for the selected combined position.
Fees at same price
Total buy and sell fees if you sell at the entry price, or the selected DCA position’s average entry. An unchanged price can still produce a net loss.
Explore your reward
Enable this and enter a target exit price to see profit after fees and return on capital including the buy fee. Risk / Reward of 1 : 2 means potential net profit is twice the planned loss.

Make shortcuts yours

Use + to add a shortcut or Edit shortcuts to open the side panel. Edit values, drag handles to reorder, or use the arrows. Use × to delete. Valid changes save automatically in this browser.

Adjust fees and appearance

Open Fees & settings to enter your broker’s percentage fees. Fees stay included when the section is collapsed. The header button switches light and dark mode.

Try it: Maximum loss Rp 500.000, entry 132, stop 129, buy fee 0.15%, sell fee 0.25% → 1,420 lots. Break-even rounded up: Rp 133.

The stop-price percentage shows the price move; Loss on invested capital includes fees. Actual loss can exceed the estimate if execution slips, gaps, or cannot fill.
